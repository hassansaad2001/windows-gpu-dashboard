Storage quotas

NTFS quotas charge every file a user owns on the volume. At the limit, new writes fail with “not enough disk space” while reading and deleting keep working; writes resume once usage is back under the limit. Users are warned by a pop-up in their session and an Application event-log entry. Administrators are exempt from “All users”.
